# Policy gap analysis (Phase 6)

Every gap keeps three things apart:
- **(a) UAE sources say**: cited from `uae_federal` / `emirate` chunks only.
- **(b) Global examples say**: cited from `global_example` chunks only.
- **(c) Gap**: labelled *ANALYSIS (not law)*.

Gap types: unclear responsibility · missing standard · legislative gap · coordination problem · objective conflict.

Candidate themes to test against the KB (hypotheses only, to be confirmed or rejected from retrieved text):

- Inferred sensitive / disability data derived by models.
- Automated decisions about students, and the right to human review.
- Age-appropriate design obligations for edtech apps.
- High-risk classification of AI used in education.
- Cross-border transfer of children's data by edtech providers.
- Which authority leads when consumer, data and education mandates overlap.
